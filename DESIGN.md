# FlowSight Individual design contract

## Position

FlowSight Individual is private work intelligence for the person doing the work. The landing page must lead with a working download path, then explain the product through real captures and precise claims.

## Visual system

- Page language: quiet technical software, light-first with system dark mode.
- Brand surface: off-white canvas, dark slate ink, static 56px technical grid.
- Signal color: FlowSight teal. Purple remains visible only inside authentic product screenshots.
- Typography: Manrope for display, Plus Jakarta Sans for body and controls.
- Shape rule: 16px grouped surfaces, 12px controls, full circles only for icon containers and semantic indicators.
- Product imagery: only real captures from the FlowSight desktop agent.

## Interaction

- Download is the primary action in the navigation, hero, download center, Free plan, and closing section.
- Hero download resolves Windows directly. macOS and Linux users are taken to their platform choices.
- Motion communicates hierarchy, product-view changes, and button feedback. It is removed when reduced motion is requested.
- All interactive targets are at least 44px on touch layouts and retain visible focus treatment.

## Content constraints

- Do not imply that cloud processing is required for Free local mode.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer outcomes, certifications, or benchmarks.
- Free and Individual Pro capabilities must match the product repository and current pricing source.
- Keep the landing in English until localization is explicitly scoped.
